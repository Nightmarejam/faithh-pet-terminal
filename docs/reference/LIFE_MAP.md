### Daily Operating Architecture for ADHD

A minimal-friction daily routine system designed specifically for ADHD — relies on good system design rather than motivation or willpower.

**Core principles:**
- **Single anchor habit** — one non-negotiable entry point to the day (e.g., coffee + 5-min review)
- **Modular loops** — reusable micro-routines for food, housekeeping, hygiene that snap together
- **Predictable rewards** — built-in dopamine hits at each transition point
- **Minimal cognitive steps** — each module has ≤3 decisions; automate the rest
- **Energy-aware scheduling** — match task difficulty to natural energy curves, not clock time

**Anti-patterns to avoid:** Overly ambitious morning routines, reliance on motivation, guilt-based accountability, rigid scheduling that breaks on one missed step.

### Life Stability Strategic Framework

Phased approach to building life stability through defined pillars, bottleneck identification, and sequenced execution.

**Pillars:** Income → Health → Housing → Relationships → Purpose (in priority order)

**Method:**
1. Identify which pillar is the current bottleneck (usually income or health)
2. Focus 80% effort on the bottleneck pillar until it's stabilized
3. Maintain other pillars at minimum viable level
4. Once bottleneck clears, reassess and shift focus to next weakest pillar

**Key insight:** Trying to optimize all pillars simultaneously leads to burnout. Sequential stabilization works better than parallel optimization for single-income/single-person households.

### Purpose Constellation Framework

Maps personal purpose not as a single "life purpose" but as a constellation of intrinsic drivers — multiple points of light that together define a navigation pattern.

**Drivers to map:**
- What activities produce flow states?
- What problems do you solve without being asked?
- What would you do even if no one was watching?
- What angers you when done badly by others?
- What do others consistently come to you for?

**Usage:** Journal responses to each question, then map the overlaps. The densest overlap zone is your "purpose cluster" — not a single point but a region of aligned energy.
