## Section 1: The Two-Plate Model

The body exists between two oscillating plates.

**Plate 1 (Top): Gravity** — a DC compression bias with oscillatory components: heartbeat ≈ 1 Hz, breath ≈ 0.2 Hz, Schumann resonance ≈ 7.8 Hz.

**Plate 2 (Bottom): Ground + somatic mass** — resonant frequencies from gait and postural sway.

**Medium:** The body’s fluid (~60% mass), with **cerebrospinal fluid (CSF)** in the cranium as the primary medium of interest.

**Key insight:** Skull sutures behave as nodal lines; bones as antinodes. The **bregma** (crown) is a central node where the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th harmonics converge — highest information density, lowest local displacement.

## Section 2: The Toroidal Loop

The system is modeled as a **closed torus**, not a linear plate.

- **Freeway space:** The ~2–3 mm interocclusal gap between teeth is a primary measurement node. Closing it (e.g. bruxism) collapses standing-wave geometry.
- **TMJ / malleus:** The discomalleolar ligament links the jaw disc to the middle-ear ossicle; jaw position tunes the inner ear.
- **Hyoid:** Acts as a position integrator encoding the harmonic state of the crown-to-ground loop.
- **Cervical lordosis:** A curved transmission line (wave buffer) that reduces destructive interference at the brainstem.

## Section 4: The Turbine Array

Waldeyer ring structures are treated as **active turbines**, not passive filters. Tonsillar crypts form Helmholtz-resonator arrays that extract energy from airflow via Fano-type interference (phase cancellation).

**Germinal centers:** Modeled as standing waves — dark zones (antinodes) for proliferation, light zones (nodes) for selection. Architecturally analogous to the Node of Ranvier cascade.

## Section 5: The Skull Cone

The skull is a **truncated cone**.

- **Apex (nose tip):** High-pressure focus; high environmental sensitivity.
- **Base (occiput):** Low-frequency transduction; mechanical noise filtration.
- **Acoustic transmission:** Parietal bone (~31% transmission) vs. occipital bone (~16%) — the skull acts as a pressure transformer.
