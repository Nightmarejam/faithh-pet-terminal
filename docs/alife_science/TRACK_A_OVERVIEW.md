# ALife Track A — Complexity Science

## Purpose
Pure emergence research. This track asks: *what does life do under pressure?*
Results here are intrinsically valuable regardless of Constella applications.
TempleOS-influenced computational minimalism — minimal genomes, minimal world rules,
maximum emergence.

## Success Criteria
A Track A experiment succeeds when it **discovers something surprising** —
behavior the experimenter did not predict from the rules.

## Experiments in This Track

### Exp 0 — Primordial Soup (baseline)
Stable populations, carrying capacity, energy equilibrium.
Result: Natural carrying capacity ~324 agents, avg energy ~210.

### Exp 1 — Pressure Selection
Predator pressure removes maladapted strategies. Red Queen dynamics.
Result: Shield strategies dominate under consistent wave pressure.

### Exp 2 — Stripe Formation
Spatial resource stratification. Agents self-organize into resource bands.
Result: Emergent stripe patterns in energy distribution.

### Exp 3 — The Anticipation Gap
Do agents develop genuine predictive behavior — modeling future threat states
rather than reacting to current ones?
Result: 89.2% predictive shielding stability, 74 unique rhythmic patterns.
Agents fire Shield BEFORE wave detection. Genuine anticipation confirmed.

### Exp 4 — Harmonic Interference
Spatial cognitive stratification under overlapping wave frequencies.
Do agents in the interference zone evolve to track beat frequencies?
Result: Spatial cognitive specialization emerged. Interference zone agents
developed distinct strategies from single-wave zones.

### Exp 5 — The Parasitic Emergence
Does offensive capability emerge incrementally? Parasitism before chemical warfare?
Result (drain=1.5): COLLAPSE at tick 10039. Parasites emerged tick 57, toxin tick 1840.
Neither counter-strategy reached critical mass before predator fully adapted.

## TempleOS Integration (Planned)
Terry Davis's design philosophy: divine simplicity, minimal abstraction, direct hardware.
ALife analog: can emergence be driven by genuinely minimal rule sets that still produce
complex behavior? What is the minimum genome size that permits anticipatory behavior?
Planned: shrink genome from 8 bytes to 4 bytes, observe what survives.

## Open Science Questions
- What is the minimum genome length for anticipatory behavior?
- Can agents evolve cross-agent communication without explicit signaling mechanics?
- What drives the transition from individual survival to collective strategy?
- Does the parasitic → toxin progression always require a triggering threshold?
