## 🧭 Decision Framework

### **Strategic Alignment Test**
Before committing to new projects/initiatives:
1. **Domain fit**: Which domain does this serve primarily?
2. **Synergy potential**: How does this advance other domains?
3. **Resource impact**: Time, money, energy requirements?
4. **Coherence check**: Does this align with 5-year vision?
5. **Flexibility**: How does this adapt to major changes?

### **Priority Matrix**
- **Impact vs Effort**: High impact, reasonable effort prioritized
- **Dependencies**: Foundation elements before advanced features
- **Timing**: Seasonal and market considerations
- **Energy**: Personal capacity and motivation cycles
